Who we are

A federal research centre on a university campus

NCERD is hosted by the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, and supervised by the Energy Commission of Nigeria, which lists it as the Commission's centre for research in solar and renewable energy.

Projects are carried out with staff of the University's departments, including mechanical and agricultural engineering, physics, statistics, economics and sociology, and the Centre welcomes proposals from researchers in every discipline.

Purpose

Vision, mission and mandate

Vision

A functional, globally comparable and competitive centre for research and development in renewable and alternative energy technologies, responsive to the energy and economic needs of Nigeria and beyond.

Mission

To keep the Centre at the forefront of research and development, innovation and knowledge transfer in renewable and alternative energy, energy management and the environment, in support of the Sustainable Development Goals.

Mandate

Research, development, dissemination and manpower training across renewable and non-renewable energy, with short courses, diplomas and degree programmes run with UNN departments.

Our story

How the Centre began and grew

Our home: the University of Nigeria

The University was founded by Dr. Nnamdi Azikiwe in 1955 and formally opened on 7 October 1960, as the first land-grant university in Africa. Its Nsukka campus covers 871 hectares of hilly savannah, about 80 km north of Enugu.

Source: NCERD conference venue page, ncerd.com.ng
Established in 1980, operating from 1982

An Act of the National Assembly established the Centre in 1980 along with three other research centres. It started full operation in 1982, funded directly by the Federal Government.

Sources: energyresearch.unn.edu.ng; unn.edu.ng
Early research areas

In its early years the Centre was active in solar, biomass and coal energy utilisation, energy consumption and energy policy studies, working with UNN departments of mechanical and agricultural engineering, physics, statistics, economics and sociology.

Source: unn.edu.ng
The first building

The Centre was first housed in a multi-purpose building with seven offices, a small laboratory, small mechanical and electrical workshops, a library, a seminar room and a conference centre, with a computerised solar collector test rig and an attached meteorological station.

Source: unn.edu.ng
Supervision by the Energy Commission

Decree No. 12 of 1988 and Decree No. 19 of 1989 gave the Energy Commission of Nigeria the legal mandate to supervise the research centres. The Commission now oversees a national network of energy centres.

Sources: energyresearch.unn.edu.ng; energy.gov.ng
Heads of the Centre

Heads named in published sources include Dr. C.N. Anyanwu (former Acting Director), Dr. P.E. Ugwuoke (Acting Director in the Centre's published directory) and the current Acting Director, Engr. Dr. Izuchukwu Francis Okafor.

Sources: TEA-LP; energyresearch.unn.edu.ng; energy.gov.ng
