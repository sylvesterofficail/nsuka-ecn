What each unit does

Our research units

Solar Photovoltaic

Testing PV components and systems against international standards, solar cell research, PV installations and installer training.

Unit head: Mrs. C.A. Nwanya

Biomass and Biogas

Family and commercial bio-digesters running on animal dung, farm waste and brewery effluent, and efficient biogas burners.

Unit head: Dr. (Mrs.) N.F. Oparaku

Solar Thermal Energy

Solar dryers, water heaters from 100 to over 500 litres, solar cookers, chicken brooders and incubators.

Unit head: Engr. O.N. Okala

Energy Management and Environment

Energy efficiency, management of energy resources and environmentally friendly energy production and use.

Unit head: Engr. I.F. Okafor

Wind and Meteorology

A wind data bank and an on-site weather station recording wind speed, direction and weather conditions.

Unit head: Dr. P.E. Ugwuoke

Fossil Energy

Coal briquetting: turning Nigeria's friable coal into compact, smokeless fuel for domestic use.

Unit head: Dr. I.S. Eze

Energy Information and Manpower Development

Research dissemination, skills-gap analysis and training workshops on renewable energy and efficiency.

Unit head: Dr. C.N. Anyanwu

Research Library and Information

A special library of books, journals, proceedings and periodicals on renewable and alternative energy.

National Centre for Energy Research and Development

University of Nigeria, Nsukka. A research centre of the Energy Commission of Nigeria, working on renewable and alternative energy since 1982.
